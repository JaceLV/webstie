01 / A SMALL OBJECT WITH A BIG JOB

Less scrolling.
More settling in.

Tempo Pebble is a simple, satisfying focus timer for people who want a calmer way to start. Turn the dial to choose a session, press once, and let the gentle light tell you how much time is left.

Pair it with Tempo’s optional AI helper for a realistic plan, a fresh idea, or a little help choosing what to do first. The helper runs through llama.cpp on your own computer.
